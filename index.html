What you can do here

Move around

W walks, A and D turn, S steps back, Shift runs. Drag to look around and scroll to zoom. On a phone, use the joystick and the Run button. Press F, or tap Fly, to fly over the city. Open the Map to see Jaipur from above and tap anywhere to go there.

The one key you need

Walk up to something and a button appears at the bottom. Press E, or tap it. That’s how you enter shops and towers, call lifts, sit at tables and steps, ride things and meet people.

Get around Jaipur

Stop a passing auto and drive it. Ride a scooter from the stands. Ride a camel at Jal Mahal. Take the jeep, or walk the spiral road, up to Sunset Point. Press V in a vehicle for the view from your seat.

Join the city

Menu, then “Register as an individual, business or company”. As an individual you get a profile and a spot. A business takes one of 205 free shops. An IT company takes one of 18 floors in the Jagatpura Tech Park and posts open roles.

Connect with people

Explore, then People, shows who’s online and who has joined. Walk up to someone online to wave. Open a profile and tap Connect. When they accept, you’re both told, private messages unlock, and you can see each other’s LinkedIn and other links.

Meets and clubs

Menu, then Community, or the notice boards at Badi Chaupar and in the Tech Park lobbies. Organise a meet with a date and place, join one, start a club, or join a club and its chat.

Talk

Sit at any café table to join that café’s chat. City chat is in the Menu. Shops have their own chat where the owner replies. Notifications in the Menu show connection requests, accepted connections and new messages.
